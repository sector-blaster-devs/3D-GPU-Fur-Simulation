Fluff Avatar

A furry creature that follows your head and talks when you talk. Use it in OBS or any streaming tool instead of showing your face.

Camera off.

Use it in OBS
  1. Press "Open output window". Keep that window open (it may sit behind others).
  2. In OBS add a Window Capture source and pick the "Fluff Avatar Output" window.
  3. Right-click the source, Filters, add Chroma Key, and match the background colour you chose here.
The output window shows only the creature, so your stream never shows these settings.
Fur rendering and creature: 3D GPU Fur Simulation, MIT. Face tracking: Google MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache 2.0).

Before we use your camera

Your browser will next ask to use your camera. Here is what happens if you allow it:

  • The video is processed only on this device, in this browser tab, to find the angle of your head and how open your mouth is.
  • The video is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Only those few numbers go to the output window, inside this browser.
  • To do the tracking, this page downloads Google's free face-tracking model and code (from Google and jsDelivr). Your video is not sent to them.
  • The microphone is not used.
  • Stop any time with "Stop camera", or close the tab.
